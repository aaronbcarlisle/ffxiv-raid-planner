## 1. Problem

A lead's recruiting is an active loop (post a listing, watch who asks, accept or invite, close when full), but in V2 it lives inside Settings, which is V1's body under V2 chrome (`pages/V2SettingsHost.tsx` mounts `StaticSettingsHost` unchanged):

- **The inbox is a settings sub-tab.** Pending requests reach a lead as a badge on the gear, a bell count on `/group/*` routes, and up to three attention rows on Static Home, all of which end in Settings → Recruitment → Requests.
- **Applicant fit is a stale snapshot.** `static_join_requests.fit_snapshot` is written at apply time. The SF1 engine, which explains fit with tier and reason rows, is never run from the applicant's side, so the lead sees less than the applicant saw on the Finder.
- **Listing status is cosmetic.** Discoverable means `is_public && discovery.enabled` (`routers/discovery.py:73-79`, `routers/join_requests.py:47-56`). `paused` and `closed` listings still appear in the Finder and still accept join requests, while the editor promises "Closed: hidden from Static Finder" (`DiscoveryTab.tsx:84`). The V1 overview's `STATUS_LABEL` (`RecruitmentTab.tsx:123-127`) knows only `open | limited | closed`, so `selective` and `paused` display as "Closed".
- **Section handoff races.** `openSettings({tab: 'recruitment', section})` loses to same-commit URL writes, which is why SF1c's "Post a listing" seeds `?rcsub=listing` as well (SF1 carried item b).

## 3. Page (RH1b frame; RH1c Applicants tab; RH1d Listing and Invites tabs)

- **Route:** `/group/:shareCode/recruit`, a child of the V2 group route (`App.tsx:170`), registered for both shells. Managers only (`canManage`, owner or lead); a member or viewer is replaced-navigated to `/group/:shareCode`. Under V1, `GroupRoute.tsx` replace-navigates back to `/group/:shareCode` (built RH1b, 2026-09-29: the route is not "unregistered" — `useMatch('/group/:shareCode/recruit')` plus a `Navigate` when `shell !== 'v2'` — so V1 is untouched, but by redirect rather than a missing route). Admin View As follows the usual rules. `PageTransition` keys the whole shell on `group:${shareCode}` for any `/group/:shareCode/*` path (R-RH-G "stays mounted" holds in the browser: switching recruit tabs, or between the recruit route and the static's other tabs, never remounts `TopBar`/`Spine`/`V2SettingsHost`).
- **Header:** `PageHeader` "Recruiting". Subtitle is the listing state: "Live · Open · 3 waiting", "Live · Paused · 2 still waiting", "Listing off". A status `Select` (open / selective / paused / closed) sits in the header and saves `settings.discovery.recruitmentStatus` through `updateGroup`, so a lead can flip status without entering the editor. Legacy `limited` reads as `selective`. (Built RH1d, 2026-09-29 — R-RH-L: the header `Select` renders on the Applicants and Invites tabs only; on the Listing tab it is hidden, because the Listing tab's own editor is the single write path there. The waiting count reads "N waiting" for `open`/`selective` and "N still waiting" for `paused`/`closed`, per R-RH-L.)
- **Tabs:** `Tabs` with `useUrlTabState('rtab', ['applicants', 'listing', 'invites'], 'applicants')`. `rtab` joins the URL-param list in `navPreferences.ts`. V1's `rcsub` sections map onto them: `requests → applicants`, `listing → listing`, `invitations → invites`, `overview → applicants`.
- **Applicants tab** (§4).
- **Listing tab** (built RH1d, 2026-09-29 — R-RH-L, replacing this bullet's original design): a slim status card on top — Live/Hidden (from `group.isPublic && discovery.enabled`) plus "N waiting" when live and waiting — with **no** `Select`, no completion checklist and no fill button on the card itself; those stayed out because the header's `Select` and `DiscoveryTab`'s own status cards already cover them, and a third write path would race the other two. Beneath the card, `DiscoveryTab` is hosted as-is, with its own save, preview, checklist and status controls — the editor is the single place that writes `recruitmentStatus` from this tab. Any editor fix lands in `DiscoveryTab` and reaches both shells.
- **Invites tab:** V2-native, on `stores/invitationStore.ts` and the existing `/api/static-groups/{id}/invitations` endpoints. The list shows code, role, uses (`use_count / max_uses` or unlimited), expiry, and Copy link (`/invite/{code}`). Revoke is a double-click confirm. The create form has role (`lead` offered to the owner only, matching `routers/invitations.py:115-121`), expires in 1–30 days or never, max uses 1–100 or unlimited (unlimited checked by default, V1 parity). `?create=1` opens the form on arrival, replacing `highlightCreateInvite`, and is stripped from the URL with `replace` once handled so a refresh doesn't reopen it. Link codes are the whole model; no email or user-targeted invites.
- **Empty states:** listing live and no requests at all: "No one has asked yet" with a link to the static's own Finder card. (Built RH1c, 2026-09-29: this positive empty state only renders when there is no history at all — a listing with only resolved requests shows just the Resolved toggle, not "No one has asked yet".) Listing off, paused or closed: the inbox says which and points at the header status control, since after RH-3 those states genuinely stop requests. No listing saved at all: the Applicants tab points at the Listing tab.
- **Width and mobile:** the page uses the 120rem layout, left-aligned. No mobile pass this stage, but the route must render at phone width with no horizontal scroll.

## 4. Applicants (RH1c)

Data: `GET /api/static-groups/{id}/join-requests?include_resolved=1&fit=1` (§5). Pending and `under_review` first, newest on top; a collapsed "Resolved" list (accepted, declined, cancelled) below.

A row shows:

- **Identity:** character name, job and role applied for (`selected_job` / `selected_role`, falling back to `job_interest` / `role_interest`), world, applied time. "View profile" appears when the applicant's profile visibility is `shareable` or `discoverable`.
- **Fit:** the tier `Tag` (strong / good / partial / weak / unknown) and the Finder's reason rows (role, schedule, goals, comms, bis), reusing the SF1 reason-row component rather than a copy. Times in the reason rows are in the static's listing zone, because the lead is the reader. `missing` renders the same hints as the Finder nudge ("no typical week", "no jobs").
- **Their words:** `message`, `availability_note`, `contact_discord`.
- **Full details** (built RH1c, 2026-09-29): a `Button variant="secondary" size="sm"` opens the existing `JoinRequestReviewModal` with the row's `request`, `staticName`, `groupId`, `discoverySettings` and the store handlers, so nothing the V1 dossier shows is lost. The row does **not** replace the modal; it duplicates the fields worth a glance and defers the rest. Disposition (R-RH-S, OWNER-4):

  | V1 dossier feature (`JoinRequestReviewModal.tsx`) | V2 home |
  |---|---|
  | avatar (`:226`) | row |
  | character, job/role, world, applied time | row |
  | fit (V1 snapshot labels `:123-126`) | row, live `fit` tier + reasons (R-RH-C); the modal keeps its snapshot view |
  | message, read-full-message (`:541`) | row shows the full message; no truncation |
  | availability summary; exact windows (`:814-833`) | summary on the row; windows in the modal |
  | Discord copy (`:196,:460`) | row (copy button + toast) |
  | gear snapshot / avg iLv (`:178`) | modal |
  | readiness badge (`:739-743`) | modal |
  | alt jobs (`:639-654`) | modal |
  | goal-alignment snapshot (`:784`) | modal |
  | accept-with-confirm (`:173,:1161`) | row: Accept is one click (spec §4); the modal keeps its confirm |
  | link to roster slot | row and modal |

  The parity matrix (`specs/v1-v2-parity-matrix.md`, D-69) carries a dated note pointing here.
- **Actions:** Accept, Decline (double-click confirm), Mark under review. Accept keeps today's semantics (`POST /api/join-requests/{id}/accept`: membership as `member`, message fields nulled); the row then offers "Link to roster slot" (`/link-roster`), as `JoinRequestReviewModal` does today. Action failures and successes surface as toasts (R-RH-Q), not inline text.
- **Active list membership** (built RH1c, 2026-09-29): the active (non-collapsed) list holds waiting rows (`pending`/`under_review`) **plus** accepted rows that have no roster link yet — "Accept then shows Link to roster" means the row stays in view, offering that action, until the lead links it to a roster slot. Only once linked does the row fall into the collapsed Resolved list.
- **Errors:** accepting a request another lead already resolved returns **400**, not 409 (`backend/app/routers/join_requests.py:822-826`: "Cannot accept a request that is already {status}"); the row's handler shows it as a toast and refetches so the row reflects what actually happened. (A 409 still covers the applicant-side create conflicts — already a member, already a pending request — which this page never triggers.) A request resolved elsewhere disappears on the next refetch. Closing the listing with requests waiting keeps them, and the header says "2 still waiting".

## 5. Backend (RH1a)

- **Status enforcement (RH-3).** `backend/app/services/discovery_settings.py` (built RH1a, 2026-09-29 — not inline in `routers/discovery.py`) holds the shared logic, replacing the two copies that were at `routers/discovery.py:73-79` and `routers/join_requests.py:47-56`: `normalize_status(raw)` — `"limited"` → `"selective"`, the four canonical statuses pass through, and **anything else** (missing, `None`, `""`, a non-string, or an unknown string) reads as `"open"` — plus `is_discoverable(group)` (public, `discovery.enabled is True`, normalized status in `{open, selective}`). `POST /api/static-groups/{share_code}/join-requests` returns **403** (`PermissionDenied`) when the listing isn't public-and-enabled at all, and a separate **409** with a plain reason when it is enabled but paused or closed (`join_requests.py:327-340`) — two different failure shapes for two different states, not one code for both. The duplicate-pending-request check reads `.where(JoinRequest.status.in_(["pending", "under_review"])).limit(1)` then `.scalars().first()` (`:351-360`), also 409. `GET /api/discovery/statics` drops paused/closed listings in both shells. The `recruitmentStatus` filter param keeps working on what remains, and its `.lower()` compares against the already-normalized value, so a non-string stored status can't 500 it. `frontend/src/components/static-group/JoinRequestBanner.tsx` gates the "Request to Join" button the same way: hidden unless `discovery.enabled === true` and the normalized status is `open` or `selective`; otherwise it shows "This static isn't taking requests right now." (OWNER-6).
- **Applicant fit (RH-6).** The viewer-input loader moved out of `routers/discovery.py` into `backend/app/services/finder_inputs.py` (built RH1a, 2026-09-29 — not a `finder_fit` helper; `finder_fit.py` stays pure/session-free) as `load_fit_inputs(session, user_ids, *, discoverable_only, availability_always, viewer_tz=None) -> dict[str, FitInputs]`, batched over a list of user ids rather than one at a time, with one entry per requested id (an empty `FitInputs(missing=["template","jobs"])` for a user with nothing). The discovery router calls it for `[current_user.id]` and issues the same statements as before (golden-tested). `GET /api/static-groups/{group_id}/join-requests` gains `fit: bool = Query(False)`; when set, **only rows with `status in ("pending", "under_review")` get a fit** — resolved rows carry `fit: null`, since their message/availability/Discord fields were already nulled at resolution and the applicant's consent to be fit-matched ends there (M5). Each waiting row's fit is `compute_fit_v2(...)` built from `load_fit_inputs` plus the listing's own recruit entries and timezone, with `asRole` = `selected_role` or the first `role_interest` entry. Coverage is judged with the listing's own zone as the display-zone override when the listing has one, falling back to the applicant's own display zone otherwise (M4: a missing/unloadable zone is never passed to `load_zone`). **OWNER-5 cap:** `if raw["missing"] and raw["tier"] in ("strong", "good"): raw["tier"] = "partial"` — applied only on this join-request path, never on the Finder's own fit. `item.fit = FitV2.from_raw(raw)`, where `FitV2.from_raw` is a `@classmethod` on `schemas/discovery.py`'s `FitV2` (shared with the discovery router, which now calls the same classmethod). The `fit` field is **additive** on every `JoinRequestResponse` — list, `/me/join-requests`, create, accept, decline all now carry `fit: FitV2 | None = None`, `null` unless requested. Requires `require_can_manage_members`, as the list already does. `fit_snapshot` is not written by this path.
- **Player Hub item.** `OverviewActionItem.type` (`schemas/player_overview.py:34`) gains `join_requests`. `services/player_overview.py` emits one per static the caller leads (owner or lead) with a non-zero pending-or-under-review count: title "N join requests waiting", detail the static name, href `/group/{shareCode}/recruit`. `NeedsYouCard.tsx` gains the label and icon entry (RH1c renders it).
- **V1 label fix.** `RecruitmentTab.tsx` `STATUS_LABEL` gains `selective: 'Selective'` and `paused: 'Paused'`.
- **Untouched:** invitation semantics, accept/decline/under-review/link-roster, notification types and hrefs, `discord_webhook.py`, the plugin endpoints. The listing stays JSON in `settings.discovery`, so `StaticGroupResponse.settings` is unchanged for the plugin.
- **Release note:** one public `fix` entry: closed and paused listings now leave the Finder and stop requests, the join banner says so, the overview status label is fixed; plus one `internal: true` entry for the additive `fit` field (V2 preview plumbing, as SF1's `fitV2` entry). `CURRENT_VERSION` bumps once per the `pr-checklist` rules.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

1. A `paused` or `closed` listing is absent from `GET /api/discovery/statics` for guests, V1 and V2 callers, and `POST …/join-requests` against it returns 409. An `open` or `selective` listing behaves as today; a listing with no status behaves as `open`.
2. `?fit=1` on the group join-request list returns a `FitV2` per item. (Corrected, built RH1a, 2026-09-29: the criterion as written did not hold in general — see `backend/tests/test_join_requests_fit.py`.) It equals what the applicant would get on the Finder for this listing with the same role **only when `missing == []`**: the OWNER-5 cap (`§5`) lowers a `strong`/`good` tier to `partial` whenever the applicant's inputs are incomplete at all (`missing` non-empty — no typical week, no jobs, or both), while the Finder's own cap (`finder_fit.py:425`) only lowers for a partial *schedule* specifically, so the two disagree whenever `missing` is non-empty but the schedule itself matched. An applicant with **no claimed role and no profile at all** gets `tier: "unknown"` (`test_no_role_and_no_profile_is_unknown`). An applicant with **a claimed role** (`selected_role` or `role_interest[0]`) that matches the listing's needs, but no profile, gets role `status: "match"` and an overall tier of **`partial`, not `unknown`** — the claimed role is honored even with nothing else on file, and the OWNER-5 cap then holds it at `partial` rather than letting it read `strong` (`test_claimed_role_without_a_profile`). Without `fit=1` the response equals today's (snapshot test).
3. `GET /api/player/overview` for a lead with two waiting requests in one static contains one `join_requests` item with that count and the recruit href; a member of the same static gets none.
4. Under V2, `/group/:code/recruit` renders for owner and lead, and replace-navigates a member to `/group/:code`. The route is registered for both shells; under V1 it replace-navigates to `/group/:code` unconditionally (`GroupRoute.tsx`, §3), so no V1 user reaches the recruit page.
5. The three tabs are URL-synced under `rtab`; `?rcsub=requests` on a V2 group route lands on Applicants.
6. Every `openSettings({tab: 'recruitment'})` caller lands on the route under V2 and opens the dock under V1. The V2 Settings dock shows no Recruitment tab; V1's shows it with its badge.
7. An applicant row shows tier, reason rows, message and the three actions; Accept then shows Link to roster; Decline needs a second click.
8. The header status `Select` saves and the Finder reflects it on the next query.
9. Invites: create, copy and revoke work end to end; `lead` is offered to the owner only; `?create=1` opens the form.
10. Static Home shows the Recruiting line to managers only; the Needs-you card renders the new item type.
11. V1 tests for `DiscoveryTab`, `SettingsPanel.roles`, `JoinRequestReviewModal` and the legacy e2e smoke pass unchanged.
