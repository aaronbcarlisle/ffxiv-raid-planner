**Goal:** under V2 chrome, `/profile` stops being V1's page with a second sidebar and becomes a V2-native **Player Hub**: an identity header, a five-tab bar (Overview · Characters & gear · Availability · Tracking · Sharing), and a glance-first Overview built from data the client already loads. - **R-PH1-B (tabs and URL).** Tab ids `overview | characters | availability | tracking | sharing`, labels `Overview · Characters & gear · Availability · Tracking · Sharing`. `hubTabs.ts` exports `resolveHubTab(params: URLSearchParams): { tab, canonical: boolean }` with the legacy map from spec §3 (`sync`, `jobs-gear`, `jobs`, `gear`, `characters` → `characters`; `preview`, `share` → `sharing`; `collections`, `goals` → `tracking`; `statics` → `overview`), `focus=availability` → `availability` when `tab` is absent, anything else → `overview`. Render uses the resolved tab at once (no Overview flash); when `canonical` is false an effect rewrites the URL with `replace: true` (canonical id, or no `tab` for Overview), keeping every other param.
